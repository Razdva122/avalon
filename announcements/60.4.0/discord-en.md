**Avalon-Game.com — Update 60.4.0**

A fresh look for Avalon! Here’s what’s changed since **59.0.0**.

**🏰 A redesigned lobby**
Find your next game more easily: rooms looking for players appear first, with filters, clear statuses and actions to join, watch or open a finished game. More compact room cards, tidier role previews and a refreshed mobile layout keep the essentials close at hand. Discord and the top player spotlight now have a more visible place, too.

**🗳️ A new vote table**
Open **History → Vote table** to compare votes across the game. See who approved or rejected each team, who led it and who was selected. Anonymous votes stay anonymous. The familiar event log is still available.

**👤 A clearer profile**
Updated layout for your account, avatar and preferences, with quick access to statistics, achievements and your sticker collection.

**🏆 Refreshed achievements**
Redesigned cards make progress and avatar/sticker rewards easier to read. Personal and global achievement pages now explain more clearly what their numbers represent.

**⚡ A lighter site**
Reduced initial downloads, improved loading of rules and wiki pages, and more consistent language navigation. Public pages are also better prepared for search engines.

Explore the update: https://avalon-game.com/
