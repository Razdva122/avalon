# Lady announcement consequence check

4 November 2026. A real Mordred inspection justified truth as preserving cover,
even though clearing the only partner in a two-person failed mission makes the
checker's Good claim impossible under this table's truthful-Good-bot rule.

The targeted experiment fixed that already selected partner and the actual Good
inspection result. Six fresh generations per variant used the identical saved
announcement context, `gpt-6.1-terra`, medium, one worker, and alternating variant
order. The current baseline matched the historical instruction hash. The common
developer prefix and stage-specific user suffix matched production transport.
Only one frozen consequence paragraph was appended to the treatment suffix.

| Outcome                                                     | Baseline | Addition |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | -------: | -------: |
| Announced partner Good, the original self-exposure          |      5/6 |      0/6 |
| Announced partner Evil                                      |      1/6 |      6/6 |
| Explicitly described self-exposure in the short explanation |      1/6 |      4/6 |
| Claimed truth preserved trust                               |      5/6 |      0/6 |

One further treatment explanation expressed the relevant Good-checker
counterfactual indirectly; another did not explicitly state the contradiction.
Short explanations were classified with variant labels hidden before unblinding.
Absence from a short explanation does not establish absence of understanding.
All 12 responses completed without retries or invalid silent-stage admissions.

An earlier six-position study produced no instances of the exact error in either
variant, so could not demonstrate an improvement. That study included a completed
normalized-transport panel and a completed production-transport panel, each with
48 responses, plus a separate incomplete pilot affected by lost output. Those
results are not pooled with this targeted announcement test.

The shipped paragraph has SHA-256
`4ce8a3033631ccb1d2533531d4092a86454ded6c14a1ada5ea5c731014403479`.
It is appended only to Evil inspection and announcement requests, outside the
common cached prefix, in the same position as the experiment. It compares public
self-exposure and future holder value without banning a target, forcing a lie or
removing deliberate sacrifices of cover for a concrete winning benefit.

These are repeated generations on one position, not independent games or a
win-rate experiment. Calling the partner Evil avoids this specific public
contradiction but remains a bluff with other costs. Private context and raw
experiment artifacts remain outside Git; no private player identifiers or
production infrastructure details are retained here.
